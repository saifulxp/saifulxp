/01 — Overview

A coordinated print collection designed to keep a brand visible in everyday spaces.

This concept project brings together a custom calendar, branded notebook and promotional banner. A consistent visual system connects the three formats through clear typography, a confident colour palette and practical layouts ready for print production.

/02 — Calendar design

A useful format with a clear brand presence.

The calendar concept balances readable date grids, seasonal imagery and brand details. The layout keeps key information easy to scan while giving each month a consistent visual rhythm.

Calendar layoutPrint design demo
/03 — Notebook design

Everyday stationery shaped by a consistent identity.

The notebook uses a restrained cover, considered spacing and coordinated inside-page details. The system can be adapted to different sizes, paper stocks and binding options without losing its recognisable brand language.

/04 — Banner design

Promotional graphics built to communicate at a glance.

The banner concept applies the same visual identity to a larger format, prioritising a strong headline, simple supporting information and high-contrast composition for visibility from a distance.
