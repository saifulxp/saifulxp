/01 — Overview

A cohesive corporate identity, designed to make every business touchpoint feel connected.

This concept project explores corporate branding and stationery design through a consistent logo system, refined typography, a confident color palette and practical branded materials. The visual identity is carried across business cards, letterheads, envelopes, presentation folders and everyday company documents.

/02 — Stationery system

Everyday business materials, unified by one clear identity.

Business cards, letterheads and envelopes share a common typographic hierarchy, spacing system and restrained palette. Each application is designed to feel professional on its own while remaining unmistakably part of the same brand.

Stationery presentationDemo concept
/03 — Brand applications

A flexible visual toolkit built for consistent communication.

Typography, color, graphic elements and layout rules work together across the stationery suite. These demo visuals are placeholders; replace images 01–11 with your own project artwork while keeping the filenames unchanged.

/04 — Brand consistency

A recognisable brand across every point of contact.

The system is designed to scale from a small business card to larger presentation materials without losing its clarity. Consistent details help every interaction feel considered, polished and connected.
